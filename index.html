About Me

Me with a cow
I am a Postdoctoral Research Scientist in Dr. Bianca Dumitrascu's group at Columbia University in the Statistics Department. I am also affiliated with the Irving Institute for Cancer Dynamics. I completed my PhD at Johns Hopkins University in the department of Applied Mathematics and Statistics under the supervision of Dr. Soledad Villar. I earned my bachelors in Computer Science and Mathematics at Rensselaer Polytechnic Institute. Additionally, I spent time in industry as a Senior Software Engineer at the foodservice startup Cut+Dry.
My current research interests are in applying machine learning methods that leverage geometric structure and symmetries in data to biological problems, particularly the mechanical interactions of cells during embryonic development. I have a background in equivariant machine learning, and my dissertation is titled "Learning O(d)-Equivariant Cartesian Tensor Functions".
Outside of work I enjoy playing volleyball, watching movies, and reading the news. You can find me at wg2492 (at) columbia.edu, or in my office 931 in the School of Social Work building, 1255 Amsterdam Ave, New York, NY 10027.
